# Compound Criteria — Clinical Sign-off Requests

**For:** CRR working group (James / clinical reviewers) · **From:** CRR digital
programme (Gary Ireland) · **Date:** July 2026 · **One page**

## What we're doing, in brief

About 60 of the 473 criteria items are "compound" — they contain several
sub-requirements joined by AND/OR (e.g. *suspected DVT **and one of**: Wells ≥2,
or Wells <2 with positive D-dimer*). Today the tools show these as one block of
text. We are restructuring them so each sub-requirement can be shown, ticked,
and assessed separately. **The wording and clinical meaning must not change** —
that is what we need you to confirm.

## What we need from you

**1. Consistency check — every restructured item (all ~60).**
For each item you'll see the current published wording next to its
sub-requirement breakdown. Please confirm the breakdown says exactly what the
wording says — nothing added, nothing dropped, no requirement made stronger or
weaker. This is a read-and-confirm exercise, a few minutes per item.

**2. Specific rulings — six items where the source wording is ambiguous or
flawed:**

| Criterion | The question for you |
|---|---|
| Paediatric hip x-ray — suspected DDH over 4 months (limited abduction; leg length discrepancy; waddling gait) | The wording doesn't say whether **any one** sign or **all** signs are needed. Our working assumption is any one sign suffices. Please rule. |
| CT head — first seizure in patient 21 or older | The published wording contains an accidental duplicated phrase. We've drafted a corrected version — please confirm it reads as intended. |
| Paediatric hip ultrasound — abnormal examination under 5 months | This entry appears to be two separate criteria run together (abnormal examination; risk-factor screening). Should it be split into two, or kept as one? |
| CT chest/abdomen/pelvis — suspected malignancy with 2+ abnormal blood tests | We plan to keep "two or more of: raised CRP, low Hb, raised calcium, high platelets, high ALP, low albumin" as a single line the referrer confirms. Alternative: list the six tests as individually tickable. Which do you prefer? |
| Paediatric hip/pelvis x-ray — DDH risk factors (footnote list) | The risk factors live in a footnote. Should they be spelled out as tickable options within the criterion, or stay as a footnote reference? |
| Each "family" below | See section 3. |

**3. Family mergers — three groups of near-duplicate items.**
When the criteria were loaded from the national PDF, three criteria got split
into many near-identical entries with the same sentence repeated in each. We
propose merging each family back into the small number of criteria the PDF
intended. Each merger needs your yes/no — none proceeds without it.

| Family | Today | Proposed | What the merge does |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT colonography | 11 entries, each repeating "able to tolerate bowel prep… no colonoscopy/CTC in the last 5 years" | 2 entries (one urgent/2-week, one 6-week) | The bowel-prep/no-recent-scope requirement appears once as a shared precondition; the presenting indications become a pick-list under it. Includes the "specialist advises" routes, which in the source also carry the precondition. |
| CT chest/abdomen/pelvis | 3 entries, two repeating the "strong suspicion of malignancy after full workup" preamble | 2 entries | The two preamble-carrying entries merge (preamble once, two qualifying routes under it). The "specialist advises" entry **stays separate** — in the source it does not carry the preamble, and merging it would wrongly impose one. |
| Chest x-ray — lung cancer symptoms | 12 entries, each repeating "one or more of the following symptoms or signs concerning for lung cancer" | 1 entry | The stem appears once; the twelve symptoms/signs become one any-of list — matching how the PDF presents it. |

Timeframes/priorities are unaffected by the mergers — urgent and non-urgent
entries are never combined.

## What happens with your sign-off

Restructured items go live in stages (mergers and complex items first), each
stage only after its sign-offs are in. Anything not signed off stays exactly
as it is today. Every change is reversible.
