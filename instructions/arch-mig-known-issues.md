# ARCH-MIG-01 — Known-issues register and disposition

**Brief:** ARCH-MIG-01 v1.0.0 · **Phase:** 1 · **Date:** 5 September 2026 · **Status:** for review (with `arch-mig-gap-analysis.md`)
**Rule applied:** the backlog is a list of problems, not of work. Each row names the *recorded fix* (what a document says should be done) and the *target fix* (what resolves it in the rules-bundle architecture). A recorded fix the target supersedes is **not implemented**. Decision D1 (tabletop runs on the pipeline; no date) means **no interim fixes are built** — every "superseded" row is retired unbuilt.

**Statuses:** RESOLVED BY DESIGN (the failure cannot occur in the target) · MITIGATED (reduced; residual stated) · OPEN (migration does not address it; owner named) · OUT OF SCOPE (reason) · CARRY (unchanged, still required).
**Verification column** names the scenario, test or build check that demonstrates the disposition; "—" means it is a policy/process item with no test.

Sources: `documents/SECURITY_DECISIONS.md` (SD/SR), `SECURITY-AUDIT-REPORT.md` (May), `documents/verification-report-2026-08.md`, `documents/DOC-AUDIT-2026-09.md`, `documents/PROJ-AUDIT-2026-07.md`, `documents/CRITERIA-REPUBLISH-2026-07-23-INVESTIGATION.md`, `instructions/ta-src-phase0-findings.md`, `instructions/compound-criteria-phase0-findings.md`, `documents/CRR_Test_Case_Results_Matrix_v2.xlsx` (37 cases), NAIAEAG summary (project doc). Not in the repo and not supplied: evaluator-level findings beyond the matrix, AI register entry, PTA, working-group record, next-steps annex, BRD v3.1.1 — rows from those are taken from the NAIAEAG summary and marked (NAIAEAG).
